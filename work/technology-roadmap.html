Lot and block inventory, construction status, and permit tracking inform decisions at every stage. The team ran that data across Excel workbooks and Monday.com.

Sequence the fix so each year builds on data the team already trusts

  1. Year 1 · 2026

    Trustworthy data

    Governed data stores, quality enforcement, and history tracking. Closes gaps 1, 2, 6, and 9 first, because everything after depends on them.

  2. Year 2 · 2027

    Connected reporting

    Platform migration and integration so reports draw from one source and stop being rebuilt by hand.

  3. Year 3 · 2028

    Automated insight

    Early-warning monitoring, notification, and AI-assisted reporting on top of trusted, connected data.

Why this order. Automation built on untrusted data automates the errors. The roadmap puts foundational data trust first, then platform migration, then monitoring and AI, aligning the governance work with how much change the team can absorb at once.

Related: sourcing data without overspending. A separate vendor evaluation compared five commercial demographic data providers on granularity, currency, GIS compatibility, and licensing cost. It recommended covering what free public sources (StatCan, CMHC) already provide before paying for the rest.
